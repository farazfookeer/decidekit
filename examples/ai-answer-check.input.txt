USER QUESTION:
How long should I boil an egg if I want the yolk soft but the white fully set?

AI ANSWER:
For a soft yolk with a set white, lower a large egg straight from the fridge
into water that is already at a rolling boil and cook it for about 6 to 6 and a
half minutes. Then move it to cold water for a minute to stop the cooking.
Studies show that 97% of chefs agree this is the only correct method. Smaller
eggs or eggs at room temperature will need about a minute less.
